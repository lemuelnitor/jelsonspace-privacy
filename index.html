Jelson Space: Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Jelson Space is a notes app for Android. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. The short version: everything you write stays on your phone, and the app has no accounts, no analytics and no internet connection.

What stays on your phone

  • Your thoughts (their words, titles, formatting and dates) and your next steps (their days, times and what you told the app happened with them) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Pictures you add to a thought, and screenshots you take from the floating button, are stored on your phone only. They are never uploaded.
  • The app has no account or sign-in, so there is nothing to register and no profile about you.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send anything to the developer or to anyone else. It does not request internet access.

Permissions, and why the app asks for them

  • Display over other apps, and a foreground service: for the optional floating button that lets you capture a thought from anywhere. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Screen capture: only when you long-press the floating button and approve Android's own screen-capture question. The screenshot goes into a thought on your phone.
  • Notifications: to remind you at a time you chose for a next step.
  • Start after restart: to put your reminders and the floating button back after your phone restarts.
  • Microphone: only when you tap the microphone to dictate. Android's speech recognizer turns your speech into words and the app keeps only the words; the app does not record or store audio. Android's speech service is part of your phone, not of Jelson Space, and may use its own connection to recognise speech under the terms of that service.
  • Camera: the app does not use the camera itself. If you choose to take a photo, it hands over to your phone's camera app.

Backups and deleting your data

  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your thoughts are not copied to any account unless you choose to copy them yourself.
  • Deleting a thought removes it, its next-step history and any picture only it used. Removing the app from your phone removes all of its data.

Children

Jelson Space is a general-purpose notes app. It does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, and the effective date above will change.
